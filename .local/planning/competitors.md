# Competitor Landscape — EveryATH

Research snapshot (Oct 2026) of other stock scanning/screening/alert products a trader might use instead of, or alongside, everyath.com. Grouped by how directly they compete with the ATH-breakout niche described in [positioning.md](../marketing/positioning.md).

## 1. Direct — ATH / Breakout-Focused Scanners

These compete most closely with our Phase 1 wedge (ATH breakouts specifically).

| Name | What it does | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Breakout Scanner](https://breakoutscanner.com/) | AI-powered breakout detection; plain-English AI screener, scans 21 global markets + ETFs, backtesting, daily alerts | Closest direct competitor — same "AI explains the setup" angle we're planning for Phase 3 |
| [Stock Alarm (Pro)](https://pro.stockalarm.io/) | Real-time alerts terminal incl. dedicated ATH/52-week-high breakout alert guides, free tier for individuals | Content marketing (blog) is strong — worth watching their SEO/content strategy |

| [ADVFN Trader Alerts](https://www.advfn.com/common/marketing/registration/insights/trader_alerts) | NYSE/NASDAQ/OTCBB/Pink Sheet breakout scanner | Legacy player, broad market coverage incl. small/micro-cap |

## 5. Where We Differentiate

Per [positioning.md](../marketing/positioning.md) section 4, most competitors above stop at "here's a list of tickers that match a filter." Few combine:
1. A **narrow, trustworthy wedge** (ATH breakouts specifically, not a 50-filter kitchen sink) — most incumbents (Finviz, TradingView, Trade Ideas) are general-purpose and require the user to build/configure the scan themselves.
2. **Plain-English explanation per signal** (what happened / why it matters / how strong / what to watch) — only Breakout Scanner and ChartingLens are clearly moving this direction; this is the space to own before they do.
3. **Simple pricing/onboarding** for a trader who just wants "tell me when a stock breaks its all-time high and why," vs. feature-heavy terminals (Trade Ideas, Benzinga Pro) that require real onboarding effort.
