# X.com (Twitter) Marketing Plan — EveryATH

Goal: drive traders/"FinTwit" to visit and sign up at everyath.com, using X as the primary acquisition channel.

This plan follows the brand and phasing from [positioning.md](../marketing/positioning.md): lead with the ATH-breakout niche (Phase 1) before broadening to Golden Cross / momentum / reversals (Phase 2).

## 1. Audience & Positioning on X

**Who we're targeting:** retail swing/momentum traders, breakout traders, FinTwit chart-posters, small-cap/large-cap momentum communities, people who follow accounts like unusual-volume scanners, breakout screeners, and "stock of the day" threads.

**What they want from a scanner account:** fast signal, proof it works, no fluff, charts not paragraphs, a reason to trust the call (volume, trend, risk).

**Our angle:** "Every ATH. Every Opportunity." — we're the account/tool that catches all-time-high breakouts the moment they happen, with a plain-English reason why. Not "another indicator bot" — a discovery feed.

**Profile setup:**
- Handle: @EveryATH (or closest available)
- Name: EveryATH — ATH Breakout Scanner
- Bio: one line on what it does + who it's for + CTA. Example: "Catching every stock hitting new all-time highs, with volume confirmation & plain-English why. Free scanner → link below."
- Link in bio: everyath.com (use a direct link, not a linktree, once the landing page has a clear CTA)
- Pinned post: a single strong example — a real ATH breakout call with the "what happened / why it matters / how strong / what to watch" format, and a CTA to the site.
- Banner: visual mockup of the product (ATH breakout card / alert example).

## 2. Content Pillars

Build everything around 4 repeatable pillars so the account doesn't rely on constant new ideas:

| Pillar | Format | Cadence | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Daily ATH Alerts** | Short post, 1 chart image per stock, native screenshot | Daily (market hours / after close) | Core value, proof of signal, habit-forming |
| **Breakout Breakdown** | Thread: What happened → Why it matters → Strength → What to watch | 2-3x/week | Differentiation — shows the "explain" layer, not just a ticker list |
| **Build in Public** | Behind-the-scenes: how the engine works, new features, data/architecture teasers | 1x/week | Builds trust, attracts technical/quant-curious traders, humanizes the brand |
| **Track Record / Recap** | Weekly thread recapping the week's ATH calls with after-the-fact price action | Weekly (e.g. Friday/weekend) | Social proof, credibility, retweet bait |

Secondary/opportunistic:
- Reacting to market-wide ATH days (e.g., "14 stocks hit new all-time highs today — here's the list") — these can go viral on big up days.
- Replying with relevant data when a big FinTwit account posts about a breakout stock (adds value, exposes account to their audience).

## 3. Posting Format Rules (X algorithm practical notes)

- **Never put the external link in the main post text** — X's algorithm suppresses reach on posts with outbound links. Put the link in the first reply, or just rely on bio link + pinned post.
- **Native images/charts outperform text-only posts.** Always attach a chart screenshot (price + volume, ATH marked) rather than describing it in words.
- **Threads outperform single long posts** for anything explanatory (Breakout Breakdown pillar). Hook in tweet 1, payoff + CTA in the last tweet.
- Post consistently at the same times (e.g., right after market open and right after close) so the audience learns when to check.
- Reply to your own big posts with the "why/how it works" follow-up — reply threads get algorithmic boost from engagement on the root post.

## 4. Growth Tactics

1. **Reply strategy ("value-add replying"):** Daily, reply with genuinely useful info (e.g., "this one's up 4% off a golden cross too, here's the chart") on 5-10 posts from larger FinTwit accounts (10k+ followers) that mention a stock we're also tracking. This is the highest-leverage, zero-cost growth tactic — it puts the account in front of audiences we don't have yet.
2. **Micro-influencer collabs:** Identify 5-10 mid-size FinTwit accounts (5k-50k followers) in the breakout/swing-trading niche. Offer free premium access in exchange for them trying it and posting an honest reaction/screenshot.
3. **Spaces:** Join or co-host X Spaces around market open/close on big trend days; mention the tool naturally when relevant (not as an ad).
4. **Big-move hijacking:** On days with major market moves (new highs on SPY/QQQ, a hot sector), post our own "today's ATH breakout list" — timely, shareable content that rides existing search/attention.
5. **Waitlist / lead magnet:** Give X-only visitors a specific reason to click through now — e.g., "first 100 signups get the Golden Cross strategy free when it launches" — rather than a generic "check out our site."
6. **Consistency over virality:** The daily alert pillar is what turns followers into habitual visitors. Don't skip days — reliability is the product's main value prop and it needs to show in the feed too.

## 5. Paid (optional, later)

Hold off on paid ads until organic content proves what resonates (which pillar gets saves/shares). Once validated:
- Promote the single best-performing organic post (not a generic ad) — cheaper and more credible.
- Target: engagement with FinTwit/stock-trading accounts, interests in "swing trading," "technical analysis," "day trading."

## 6. Metrics to Track

- Link clicks from X → everyath.com (use a tracked/UTM link: `everyath.com/?utm_source=x`)
- Follower growth rate (secondary — not the goal, clicks/signups are)
- Signups attributed to X (compare against other channels)
- Engagement rate per pillar (which content type actually drives profile visits / link clicks, not just likes)
- Weekly recap post performance (track record posts are the credibility engine — watch save/share rate specifically)

## 7. 30-Day Launch Plan

**Week 1 — Setup & seed content**
- Finish profile (bio, pinned post, banner)
- Post 5-7 days of Daily ATH Alerts to build a content backlog/history before pushing for followers
- Start replying to 5+ larger accounts/day

**Week 2 — Differentiation content**
- Launch Breakout Breakdown thread format (2-3 this week)
- First Build in Public post (explain the strategy engine / why ATH + volume confirmation matters)
- Reach out to 3-5 micro-influencers for early access

**Week 3 — Social proof**
- First weekly Track Record recap thread
- Push UTM-tracked link harder in pinned post / bio once there's a week of real track record to point to
- Evaluate which pillar is driving the most link clicks; double down on it

**Week 4 — Scale what works**
- Increase cadence on best-performing pillar
- Consider first Spaces appearance
- Decide go/no-go on paid promotion of the top organic post

## Notes

- Keep this plan scoped to **Phase 1 (ATH breakouts)** per [positioning.md](../marketing/positioning.md) — don't introduce Golden Cross / momentum content on X until it ships in-product, to avoid promising more than the site currently delivers.
- Revisit this plan once Phase 2 features ship — new pillars (Golden Cross Breakdown, Momentum/Reversal calls) slot into the same structure.
